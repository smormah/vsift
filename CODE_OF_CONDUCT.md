# Code of conduct

VSift is committed to a welcoming, safe, and professional open-source community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different levels of experience.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Protect private information and never publish another person's sensitive media or data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, or publication of private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, impact, prior behaviour, and the safety of affected participants. Good-faith reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
