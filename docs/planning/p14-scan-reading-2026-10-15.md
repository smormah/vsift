# P14 scan reading, 2026-10-15 (RQ-13, R-SEC03), for the release candidate 0.2.1-rc.3

Status: a dated record of what was read on 2026-10-15, two days after `0.2.1-rc.3` was published (publish run 38457712965, 2026-10-13), at `main`
`fa380021f2aa` (the tag `v0.2.1-rc.3`'s commit; the records change #398 was open and added only work records), for the candidate that the stable `0.2.1` will be cut from. It
repeats the method of [`p14-scan-reading-2026-10-10.md`](p14-scan-reading-2026-10-10.md) for the published `0.2.0`, which follows
[`-10-08`](p14-scan-reading-2026-10-08.md), [`-10-07`](p14-scan-reading-2026-10-07.md), [`-10-05`](p14-scan-reading-2026-10-05.md) and
[`-10-02`](p14-scan-reading-2026-10-02.md) with its addendum of 2026-10-04; this record does not repeat their method sections. It reads only public sources,
with the tools' own default clients. Nothing was dismissed, closed, patched or changed in any setting, the FFmpeg and whisper.cpp catalogue was not touched, nobody at the FFmpeg or
whisper.cpp projects was contacted, nothing was posted or commented anywhere upstream, and nothing was installed on a developer machine. **The reading did open two issues in this
repository, #399 and #400, one per new finding (the rule that a finding becomes a tracked issue); both carried a severity and a proposed disposition, and the maintainer decided both on 2026-10-15 (below).**

**Result: RQ-13 is recorded `passed` for `0.2.1-rc.3`, with two new findings named and decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-15; everything else agrees with 2026-10-10.** The reading was first written with the item `running` and the two findings undecided; the maintainer's decisions (in chat, 2026-10-15) were added the same day. The item's pass rule (plan section 6) is no
open high or critical finding on a supported path unless fixed, mitigated, or accepted by the maintainer with a register entry, and a disposition that accepts a risk is the maintainer's to make: with #399 accepted (L-137) and #400 closed as fixed by inference with that residual accepted (L-122), no finding is left undecided.

1. **[#399](https://github.com/smormah/vsift/issues/399): CVE-2026-110810 names the pinned whisper.cpp.** Published at the National Vulnerability Database on 2026-10-15 01:19 UTC (status "Received"; source VulDB), Low
   (CVSS 4.0 0.9, 3.1 2.8): an out-of-bounds read in the model loader (`whisper_model_load`) from an unvalidated tensor type in a model file, whisper.cpp "up to 1.9.4", fixed by `60c0be6ac8` (2026-10-02, in v1.9.5, not in v1.9.2). The pin is v1.9.2, so it is in range and unfixed. It is the change
   [#322](https://github.com/smormah/vsift/issues/322) already listed on 2026-10-07 as a model-loader hardening commit ("a tensor type check"); it is the first record for that family. VSift does not reach it in use: a user may register another model file, but the engine hashes the model and refuses one that is not a reviewed pin before any chunk is recognised (`LocalAsrModelNotPinned`), so the loader runs only on the two pinned, hash-checked models, which leaves the local swap between hashing and loading that [L-137](known-limits.md#l-137) already names. **The maintainer's decision, 2026-10-15: accepted for `0.2.1` under L-137, because only a reviewed pinned model is loaded; fixed with the planned whisper.cpp re-pin; #399 stays open until then.** (The reading recommended exactly this.)
2. **[#400](https://github.com/smormah/vsift/issues/400): CVE-2026-109311, a record filed against vLLM, describes a heap overflow in FFmpeg's IAMF parser that VSift's MP4 demuxer can reach.** Published 2026-10-12 17:17 UTC (status "Received"; source VulnCheck), CVSS 4.0 7.1 (High), 3.1 6.5 (Medium); the linked project advisory rates it Moderate. It names no FFmpeg commit and has no FFmpeg CVE, so the ancestry tool cannot tie it to a fix. By source: the site it names (`scalable_channel_layout_config()` in `libavformat/iamf_parse.c`) has, in the shipped snapshot, a channel-count consistency check added by `f8e6f8f0a2` (2025-12-27, "Invalid write of size 4"), which is an ancestor of the snapshot, and the advisory's own version gate treats libavformat 63 and later as outside the range (the snapshot is 63.1.101). **It is fixed in the shipped build by inference, not by name, nothing was run, and its record is High by CVSS 4.0: treated as high until the maintainer closes it, as [#272](https://github.com/smormah/vsift/issues/272) was.** **The maintainer's decision, 2026-10-15: closed as fixed by inference, recorded beside [L-122](known-limits.md#l-122)** (the check at the named site is present in the shipped snapshot and `f8e6f8f0a2` is an ancestor); the residual that the fix is tied to the record by inference and not by name is accepted, as L-122's is; the 2026-10-31 re-pin strengthens it. #400 is closed. No mutant was run.

**Standing acceptances, restated:** the maintainer's acceptances of CVE-2026-107678 ([L-144](known-limits.md#l-144), [#351](https://github.com/smormah/vsift/issues/351), 2026-10-10), CVE-2026-38350

## What it does not show, and what is weaker than it sounds

- **A reading of public records and public history, not a test of a binary.** Nothing ran FFmpeg or whisper.cpp on a crafted input. The stream of records has not slowed: 14 new FFmpeg-keyword records in seven days, one of them reaching VSift's parser.
- **The records are a sample, and the freshest are the least analysed.** CVE-2026-109311 and CVE-2026-110810 are "Received": the scores are the reporters', and a record may be wrong about the component or the version. Records published before 2026-06-01 were read only where they moved (CVE-2025-63757).
- **The hosted run's cargo verdicts are the log's two lines**; the job's artifact (the advisory JSON, the duplicate-version notes, the binary and SBOM files) was not downloaded; the by-hand `cargo deny` of the day covers the advisory verdict.
- **Gaps carried over:** the worker image's packages are unscanned; the published Windows and macOS executables' system library requirements were not read (only Linux); the Homebrew tools of the macOS journeys and the pinned Windows tools are outside this reading; the native tools are not in the SBOM or in `cargo deny`; the BtbN build's configure flags were not re-read (that the IAMF decoder is enabled is assumed, as on 2026-10-02).
- **A status, not a review of every alert:** zero alerts in a store that has been empty throughout shows no alert was raised, not that the code is free of findings.

and a read of this run's log. **FFmpeg's code host** (`code.ffmpeg.org`): five requests (the two pull requests, one comment list, one diff and one page). **VulnCheck** (`vulncheck.com`): one page request for CVE-2026-109311's advisory (saved, not used). **The Node.js project** (`nodejs.org`): the release index, one request. **npm's registry** (`npm view`): a few requests for `npm` dist-tags, deprecation and publish times. **The Docker registry**: one anonymous token request (`auth.docker.io`) and one manifest header request (`registry-1.docker.io`) for `ubuntu:24.04`. The advisory database of `cargo deny` was fetched by the tool's own client. The saved responses are in a scratch folder outside the repository and are not committed.
