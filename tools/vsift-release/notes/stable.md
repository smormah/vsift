## What this release promises

The `vsift` command-line grammar, its exit codes and its v1 JSON contracts. They change only by addition: new commands, fields and failure codes may appear, and what exists keeps its meaning. Nothing else is promised. The accuracy figures were measured on a synthetic corpus; what was tried, and what was not, is in the installation guide and in the register of known limits (https://github.com/{repository}/blob/{tag}/docs/planning/known-limits.md).
